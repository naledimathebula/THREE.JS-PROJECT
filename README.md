# Chicken Licken Flame Bar 🔥
### Turning the Heat of Hot Wings into an Interactive Experience

## Project Overview

**Chicken Licken Flame Bar** is a creative technology concept inspired by the heat of Chicken Licken's Hot Wings. The idea was to transform the experience of eating spicy chicken into an interactive installation where the intensity of the heat becomes something people can express, experience, and share.

Inspired by the kind of innovative brand experiences I imagined creating at **Joe Public**, I envisioned an installation that connects food, technology, and human interaction to bring a brand to life in a memorable way.

## The Concept

What if the heat of your Hot Wings could be measured through your voice?

The Flame Bar explores the idea of using a microphone as an interactive input to represent a person's reaction to spicy food. As participants experience the heat of Chicken Licken's Hot Wings, their vocal reactions could influence a visual flame or heat meter, creating a playful interpretation of their spice tolerance.

The concept turns an everyday food experience into an opportunity for participation, competition, and brand engagement.

## The Creative Challenge

One of the biggest challenges was exploring **how to use a microphone as a taste bar**.

A microphone cannot directly measure taste or the physical heat of chilli. Instead, it can capture sound levels and vocal reactions that could be translated into a visual representation of perceived heat.

This presented an interesting creative technology challenge: how could I connect a sensory experience, such as eating spicy food, with an interactive digital system?

The idea encouraged me to think beyond traditional screens and interfaces, exploring how human behaviour could become an input for a more engaging brand experience.

## Creative Technology Approach

The concept brings together:

- **Brand Experience:** Creating an interactive activation inspired by Chicken Licken's Hot Wings.
- **Creative Coding:** Exploring how microphone input could drive real-time visual feedback.
- **Interaction Design:** Turning participants' reactions into an engaging and intuitive experience.
- **Experiential Marketing:** Encouraging participation, social interaction, and memorable brand engagement.
- **Creative Problem-Solving:** Investigating how an everyday sensory experience could inspire an unconventional digital interaction.

## The Vision

The vision for Flame Bar was to create more than a digital display. It was to imagine a brand experience that people could physically participate in, react to, and remember.

By combining food culture, sound, visualisation, and interactive technology, the concept explores how creative technology can turn a simple brand interaction into an experience.

## What I Learned

This concept challenged me to think about the relationship between human senses and technology. It pushed me to consider how to translate an abstract experience, such as the perceived heat of spicy food, into an interactive digital response.

It also reinforced my interest in working at the intersection of **storytelling, design, code, and brand experience** — using technology not just to build interfaces, but to create moments that connect people with brands in unexpected ways.
